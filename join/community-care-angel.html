Community Care Angel

ԴիմելApply

Help people get value. Understand their needs. Guide their growth.

As a Community Care Angel, you are responsible for the experience, support, and success of every person already inside our ecosystem.

You are not selling — you are caring, guiding, and ensuring people get real value after they enter.

You are the reliable bridge between the community members and the products/courses:

  • You make sure no one is lost
  • No question stays unanswered
  • No opportunity for growth is missed

During active periods, you also support upsell activities — but always from a place of care and relevance, not pressure.

A critical part of this role is thinking like a doctor, not a seller: you don’t act on what people say they want — you diagnose what they actually need. You ask, listen, understand the root problem, and guide them to the best solution for their growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support users through calls and messaging across all stages of their journey
  • Diagnose real needs (not just stated wants) and guide users accordingly
  • Answer questions about courses, tools, schedules, and learning paths
  • Help users navigate platforms, materials, and community spaces
  • Ensure participants are engaged, active, and progressing
  • Identify users who can benefit from deeper learning (Level Up, Pro) and guide them accordingly
  • Handle operational and coordination tasks related to courses and community
  • Maintain clean and structured communication records
  • Report daily on activities, issues, and insights
  • Spot recurring problems and implement improvements
  • Work closely with Engagement, Education, and Operations teams

What We Need From You

  • You have 2–4 years of relevant experience (customer support, community management, operations, or similar)
  • You are patient, attentive, and a deep listener
  • You are able to understand people beyond what they say and identify their real needs
  • You communicate clearly and calmly, even under pressure
  • You can handle multiple conversations without losing quality
  • You are organized and detail-oriented
  • You are comfortable working with tools (CRM, messaging platforms, spreadsheets, AI tools — preferred)
  • You are AI-proficient or highly AI-curious, actively using tools and exploring how they improve your work
  • You constantly look for ways to use AI and technology to deliver faster, better, higher-quality care for our community members
  • You take responsibility and follow through
  • You don’t ignore problems — you solve them
  • You genuinely care about people having a good experience

What We Offer

  • A central role in shaping the user experience of AI education in Armenia
  • A fast-paced environment with real responsibility
  • The opportunity to grow into Engagement, Operations, or Product roles
  • A team that values ownership and initiative over hierarchy

Our Mission

One million AI-literate Armenians within five years.

We want to make AI practical, accessible, and useful for people across Armenia — helping individuals grow, organizations transform, and our country become a global exporter of AI transformation.
