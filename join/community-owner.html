We're not hiring someone to "manage a community." We're handing someone the keys to own one — and to make it the community thousands of Armenians are proud to belong to.

Who We Are

Lumiverse (formerly reArmenia Academy) exists to transform education. Our near-term mission is bold and specific: one million AI-literate Armenians within 5 years, and Armenia as a global exporter of knowledge and Education Transformation Globally.

AI քեզ բան is our flagship learning program, an AI-driven community of people who never stop learning, who share knowledge openly, and who help each other achieve far more than they could alone.

We are community-first, from the very core. We serve our community. When something goes wrong, we assume it's on us, and we fix it, fast, always giving priority and privilege to the member.

The Role in One Line

You own everything community. The health of it, the happiness of it, the growth of it — all of it is yours. This role has two directions:

1. Community Care

Make every member feel happy, satisfied, and genuinely excited about every service and every interaction with us. You'll map and own every touchpoint where the community needs care:

  • Messages & responses
  • Calls
  • Course onboarding
  • Feedback calls
  • …and every other stream you uncover

The standard: a member should never feel like a ticket. They should feel served.

2. Community Development

Grow the community, and grow belonging. The goal: when someone asks a member "Are you part of any communities?", they answer without hesitation — "Yes, I'm in the AI քեզ բան community" — and they bring others in.

You'll find the channels, the rituals, and the reasons that make people identify as one of us and invite others to join.

Your First Mission

You'll start by designing the foundation:

  • Define the architecture of the community — how it's structured, how people move through it, where value is created.
  • Map the core streams and decide, for each, where AI agents and automation should run the experience and where a human must be in the loop.
  • Build and orchestrate the hybrid (human + AI) team that delivers a flawless experience to every single member.

What We Need From You (Non-Negotiables)

  • Ownership — the #1 trait. You don't wait for instructions. You take full responsibility for the community and everything connected to it. It's yours.
  • Full focus. This is not an ordinary job and it cannot be a side gig. No other commitments or involvements. Your energy and ideas go here, every day, fully.
  • Care & servant leadership. You genuinely serve people. Solving problems for members — quickly, generously.
  • AI-First. You're excited by what AI makes possible and build an AI-native community that thrives.
  • Mission belief. You're passionate about making Armenia literate, strong, and flourishing through education, and you'll carry that mission alongside your teammates.
  • Language fluency. You’re fluent in Armenian, English, and Russian, both spoken and written, and communicate clearly and confidently in both.
